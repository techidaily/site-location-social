You will be to see your overall data usage. Scroll down to know how much mobile data individual apps are using. #### 3\. Have Access to Your Device's Microphone or Camera

When an app uses the microphone on iPhones, you will see an orange dot at the top of your screen and, similarly, a green dot for the camera. On Android phones, when an app starts, you will see a microphone or camera icon popup in the top right corner, which then turns into a green dot. These are healthy indicators that you should not ignore. Also, go to the list of apps that are allowed to access your ZTE Blade A73 5G's camera or microphone. If you see mSpy there, that means your phone is being spied on.
